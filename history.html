World Cup History Timeline

A few landmark moments from the history of the FIFA World Cup.

1930

The first FIFA World Cup is held in Uruguay, which also wins the tournament as host nation, beating Argentina 4-2 in the final.

1950

Brazil hosts the tournament and suffers a stunning upset loss to Uruguay in the deciding match, an event still remembered in Brazil as the "Maracanazo."

1970

Pelé leads Brazil to a third World Cup title in Mexico, with a team many consider one of the greatest in the sport's history.

1986

Diego Maradona single-handedly drags Argentina to the title, scoring both the infamous "Hand of God" goal and the "Goal of the Century" in the same match against England.

1999 - 2015

While not the men's World Cup, the U.S. Women's National Team's dominant run — including titles in 1999 and 2015 — helped grow the global profile of the Women's World Cup.

2014

Germany defeats hosts Brazil 7-1 in the semifinal, one of the most shocking results in World Cup history, before going on to win the tournament.

2022

Argentina wins the World Cup in Qatar in a dramatic final against France that goes to penalties, capped off by Lionel Messi finally lifting the trophy.
